# What's New on Indeed?

### Quick summary

We want to make it easier for you to find a job and apply. That's why we're adding new features and improving old ones. Updates may take time, so you might not see them right away. Here's what's new:

- Indeed Career Scout
- Passkeys

## Indeed Career Scout

Indeed Career Scout is a new AI tool that helps with your job search. With it, you can explore careers, practice interviewing, build a strong resume, and learn more about companies before applying.

What to expect

- Explore careers: Discover paths based on your skills, experience, and goals.
- Find better job matches: Get personalized recommendations powered by AI.
- Apply faster: Autofill applications for Top Match jobs and tailor your resume to highlight the skills that fit.
- Get interview-ready: Schedule your interviews and practice with custom tips and insights.
- Research companies: Get summaries of employee reviews, pay data, and role insights.
- Connect with employers: Get help writing messages and scheduling interviews.

## Passkeys

Passkeys are a safer, simpler way to sign in to your Indeed account. They use your device's built-in security, like a fingerprint, face scan, or screen lock. This means no more passwords to remember.

What to expect

- Easier sign-in: Use your device's face, fingerprint, or PIN to sign in.
- More secure: Passkeys protect you from phishing and replace old passwords.
- One-step verification: No need for separate two-factor codes.
- Full control: Create, manage, or delete passkeys in your account settings.

You'll still have the option to sign in with a one-time passcode if needed.
